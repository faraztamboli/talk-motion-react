Serve a Deaf customer now, without the wait.

How a counter conversation works

01

You speak normally

Your words appear on the screen as live captions.

02

They answer their way

Typing on the tablet or on their own phone.

03

They confirm

Nothing is spoken until they tap Speak.

04

You hear them

The tablet says their reply out loud.

What your team does

  • Open TalkMotion on a counter tablet and sign in.
  • Speak normally, then wait for the reply.
  • Offer the code if the customer prefers their own phone.

Staff guide: about twenty minutes, with a practice run. talk-motion.com/staff-guide

What the customer does

  • Read your words as captions.
  • Type their reply, on the tablet or their own phone.
  • Tap Speak when it says what they mean.

Customers never pay and never install anything.

Set-up

  • Any tablet or phone with a current browser, on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • No IT project. No software to install.
  • Private mode, on by default: what is said stays on our servers and these phones, and is deleted 15 minutes after each conversation.
  • Your admin adds and removes staff, and reads a monthly summary that counts conversations and never includes what was said.

What it does not do

  • It does not replace a qualified interpreter. For complex medical conversations, book one. TalkMotion helps with short exchanges and the wait until one arrives.
  • It does not read sign language in conversations. Customers type.
  • It is not a medical device and makes no clinical decisions.
  • We do not sign HIPAA business associate agreements yet. Ask your privacy officer first.
