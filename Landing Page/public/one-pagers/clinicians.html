TalkMotion
For speech-language pathologists, ENT and ALS teams

A conversation tool your patients will still use at the counter.

For patients who cannot speak, or who are Deaf. They type or tap a suggested reply, confirm it, and TalkMotion says it aloud. It also captions what the other person says. Free for patients.

Who it helps

Voice loss

Laryngectomy, vocal cord injury, tracheostomy, post-surgical voice rest.

Progressive conditions

ALS and other conditions where speech is declining but typing is still possible.

Deaf and hard of hearing

Live captions of the hearing person, with an optional plain-language version.

What it does today

  • Speaks typed replies in a natural voice, in under a second.
  • Suggests replies from what the other person just said. The patient picks, edits or ignores them.
  • Never speaks anything the patient has not confirmed.
  • Captions the other person live, so the patient is not guessing.
  • Runs in a phone browser. Nothing to install.
  • Saves a transcript the patient can keep.

What it does not do

  • It is not a medical device and is not a replacement for a prescribed AAC system.
  • It does not place phone calls or contact emergency services.
  • It needs an internet connection.
  • A personal cloned voice is not available yet. When it is, it will require the patient's recorded consent.

How to refer a patient

  • Ask the patient to open talk-motion.com on their phone and choose "Try a conversation, free".
  • On the first screen they choose "I am Deaf or can't speak".
  • Practise one short exchange together in the clinic. Most people need two or three minutes.

Patients never pay. If your clinic wants a tablet for reception, see the pharmacy and clinic page.

Why patients keep using it

Many AAC tools are used in therapy and abandoned in daily life. TalkMotion is built for the short, everyday exchange: the pharmacy, the front desk, the shop. It hears the other person, suggests a reply from what they said, and waits for the patient to say yes.
