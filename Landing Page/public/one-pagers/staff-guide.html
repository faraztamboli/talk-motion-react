TalkMotion
Staff guide for pharmacies and clinics

Twenty minutes, including a practice run.

How to serve a Deaf or non-speaking customer with TalkMotion at your counter.

1. Before your first customer

  • Create a free account at app.talk-motion.com. Ask your location's admin to add you as staff.
  • On the counter tablet, open app.talk-motion.com and sign in.
  • Put the tablet on a stand where the customer can read it. Turn the volume up.

2. A conversation

  1. Open Conversation, choose I can hear and speak, check your location, tap Start.
  2. Switch on Listen to speech. Your words appear as captions.
  3. Turn the tablet to the customer, or tap Add a phone so they join on their own phone.
  4. Speak normally, one or two sentences at a time. Then wait.
  5. The customer types or taps a reply and taps Speak. Only then is it said aloud.
  6. When you are done, tap End.

3. When something goes wrong

  • A caption is wrong: say it again in different words.
  • They prefer their own phone: tap Add a phone and show the code.
  • Health, the law or money: offer a qualified interpreter. TalkMotion does not replace one.
  • No sound: check the volume, then ask them to tap Speak again.
  • They prefer pen and paper: that is fine too.

4. Courtesy

  • Look at the customer, not only at the screen.
  • Never type or tap Speak for them. Their words are theirs.
  • Do not read their reply aloud before they tap Speak.

5. Afterwards

  • The transcript is kept under My conversations. Unless the customer asks for a copy, delete it there.
  • The monthly summary counts conversations. It never includes what anyone said.

6. Practice run (10 minutes, with a colleague)

Your colleague plays a customer picking up a new prescription, on their own phone with the code. Ask about allergies, explain how often to take it, and ask if they have questions. Then swap roles. Try one mistake on purpose: mumble a sentence and practice the repair.
