A note for interpreter agencies

We do not replace interpreters. We cover the gap when none is there.

A short note on what TalkMotion is, what it is not, and why we think it is on your side.

TalkMotion is a conversation tool for Deaf people and people who cannot speak. The hearing person's words appear as live captions. The Deaf or non-speaking person types or taps a reply, and it is spoken aloud only when they confirm it.

It is built for short, everyday exchanges where no interpreter is present: the pharmacy counter, the bank, the front desk, the late-night pickup. Those moments are not served today, and no agency can staff them.

Our policy, in writing

  • TalkMotion does not replace qualified interpreters, and we never say it does.
  • Inside every conversation, the app shows a notice: for health, legal or money matters, ask for a qualified interpreter.
  • One tap fills in "I would like a sign language interpreter, please" for the user to say.
  • We do not market TalkMotion to hospitals, courts or schools as a substitute for interpreting services.
  • TalkMotion does not read sign language in conversations. Users type or tap.

Where we think it helps you

  • The wait before an interpreter arrives, for check-in and logistics.
  • Short follow-ups after an interpreted appointment.
  • Venues that learn, through using it, that some conversations need more. That is a referral.

What we would like from you

  • Tell us where the wording or the boundaries are wrong.
  • Let us link to your booking page from inside the app, so "ask for an interpreter" leads somewhere real.
  • Nothing else. There is no fee and no contract.
