TalkMotion
An invitation to Deaf organizations

Test us. Tell us what is wrong. Hold us to it.

We are a small team building with AI. We do not speak for the Deaf community, and we are not claiming to be Deaf-led. We are asking for your scrutiny.

You have seen sign-language gloves, avatars and apps that promise translation. Most were built about Deaf people, not with them. We want to do this differently, and we know that saying so is not enough.

TalkMotion today is simple. The hearing person's words appear as live captions. The Deaf person answers by typing or tapping a suggested reply. Nothing is spoken until they tap Speak. It does not read sign language in conversations, and it does not replace interpreters.

What we have committed to

  • Nothing is spoken on a user's behalf without their tap.
  • No accuracy numbers from hearing testers. Only Deaf users judge accuracy.
  • No avatars and no generated signing.
  • A public list of every claim we make and every claim we refuse to make.
  • A monthly changelog of what people told us and what we changed.
  • Free for individual Deaf and non-speaking users.

What we are asking

  • Try it with your members and tell us what fails.
  • Tell us which words to stop using. We will.
  • If a member signs on camera, help us make ASL versions of our pages.
  • If you think we should not build something, tell us that too.

Volunteering is unpaid, and we say so plainly. We credit people by name only when they say yes.
